How to Use

1
Copy a video URL
Copy any video URL from YouTube, Vimeo, Twitter, TikTok, or 1000+ other sites
2
Paste it here
The URL will auto-detect from your clipboard, or click the paste button
3
Choose format & quality
Select MP4 (video) or MP3 (audio only), then pick your quality
4
Click download
The video will be downloaded and added directly to your Eagle library
